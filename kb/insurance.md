# Insurance

## Plans we accept
We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna Dental PPO, MetLife PDP, Guardian and Aetna Dental PPO. Plan names here are examples for this fictional demo. We do not accept HMO or DHMO plans.

## How claims work
We submit claims to your insurer for you on the day of treatment. You pay only your estimated share on the day. If the insurer pays less than estimated, we send you a statement for the difference within 30 days.

## Out-of-network insurance
If your plan is not on our list, we still treat you and give you an itemised receipt to claim back from your insurer yourself. Out-of-network reimbursement depends entirely on your plan.

## No insurance? Our membership plan
The Juniper Membership costs $299 a year for adults and $199 for children under 16. It covers two check-ups and cleanings, all routine X-rays, one emergency exam, and 15% off other treatment. There is no waiting period.

## Checking your coverage
Send us a photo of the front and back of your insurance card before your visit and we will check your coverage and remaining annual maximum for you.
