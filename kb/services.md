# Services

## Check-ups and cleanings
A routine check-up includes an exam by the dentist, a professional cleaning by a hygienist, and X-rays once a year or as needed. Most adults need one every six months.

## Fillings
We use tooth-coloured composite fillings. A small filling usually takes 30 to 45 minutes and you can eat normally once the numbness wears off.

## Crowns
A crown covers a damaged or weakened tooth. Ours are made in-house with a same-day milling machine, so most crowns are fitted in a single 2-hour visit with no temporary crown.

## Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside a tooth and seals it. Most front and premolar teeth are treated in one visit; molars sometimes need two. A crown is usually recommended afterwards.

## Dental implants
Implants replace missing teeth with a titanium post and a crown. Dr. Reyes places implants at our practice. The full process usually takes three to six months, including healing time, and starts with a consultation and a 3D scan.

## Teeth whitening
We offer in-chair whitening (about 90 minutes) and take-home whitening trays. A check-up is required first, because whitening is not suitable over untreated decay or gum disease.

## Clear aligners
Dr. Natarajan provides clear aligner treatment for teens and adults. Treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months. A free 20-minute consultation tells you whether aligners suit your teeth.

## What we do not offer
We do not provide general anaesthesia, wisdom-tooth removal under sedation, or orthodontic braces with metal brackets. We refer these to trusted specialists nearby.
