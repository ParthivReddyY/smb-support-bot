# Policies

## Privacy
Your health information is kept confidential and stored securely. We only share it with your insurer for claims, with specialists we refer you to, or when the law requires it. You can ask for a copy of your records at any time.

## Transferring records
To move your records to or from another dentist, complete a records release form at the front desk or by email. We send records within 5 working days, free of charge.

## Patients under 18
A parent or legal guardian must sign consent for treatment for patients under 18. Teenagers aged 16 and 17 may attend check-ups alone once a guardian has signed a standing consent form.

## Feedback and complaints
We want to hear when something goes wrong. Ask to speak with our practice manager, or email feedback@junipermock.example, and we will reply within 3 working days.
