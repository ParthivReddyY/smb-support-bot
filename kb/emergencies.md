# Dental emergencies

## What counts as an emergency
Severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, swelling in the face or gums, a lost crown or filling causing pain, and bleeding that will not stop after an extraction.

## Same-day emergency appointments
We hold emergency slots every weekday morning. Call (555) 014-2290 as soon as we open at 8:00 am and we will see you the same day whenever possible. The emergency exam fee is $85, plus any treatment.

## After hours
Outside opening hours, call our usual number and choose option 9 to reach the on-call dentist for existing patients. The on-call dentist can advise you and arrange to meet you at the practice when necessary.

## When to go to hospital
Go to a hospital emergency department or call 911 if you have facial swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing or that spreads towards your eye, a high fever with dental pain, a jaw injury after an accident, or bleeding you cannot control. This assistant cannot assess medical emergencies.

## Knocked-out adult tooth
Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root. If it is dirty, rinse it briefly in milk. Try to put it back in the socket, or keep it in milk, and call us immediately. A tooth has the best chance of being saved within 60 minutes.
