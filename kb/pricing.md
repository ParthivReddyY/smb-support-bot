# Prices and payment

## Prices: exams, cleanings and fillings
New-patient exam with X-rays costs $120. A check-up and cleaning costs $95. A composite (tooth-coloured) filling costs from $150. An emergency exam costs $85. All prices are fictional and for this demo only.

## Prices: crowns and root canals
A same-day crown costs $1,100. Root canal treatment costs $750 for a front tooth and $1,150 for a molar. A crown is usually recommended after a root canal. All prices are fictional and for this demo only.

## Prices: implants, whitening and aligners
A single dental implant with crown costs from $3,400. In-chair teeth whitening costs $450, and take-home whitening trays cost $280. Clear aligner treatment costs from $3,800. Nitrous oxide is $60 per visit. All prices are fictional and for this demo only.

## Written estimates
After your exam we give you a written estimate for any treatment over $200, including what we expect your insurance to cover. The price on your estimate is honoured for 90 days.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, debit cards, all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept personal cheques. Payment is due on the day of treatment for the part not covered by insurance.

## Payment plans
For treatment over $500 you can spread the cost over 3, 6 or 12 months with no interest, subject to a short eligibility check. Ask the front desk to set this up before treatment starts.

## Refunds
If you prepaid for treatment that you cancel with at least 24 hours' notice, we refund the full amount to your original payment method within 5 working days.
