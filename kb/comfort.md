# Comfort and accessibility

## Nervous patients
Many of our patients feel anxious about the dentist. We offer longer appointments, a stop signal you can use at any time, noise-cancelling headphones, and a walkthrough of each step before we start.

## Nitrous oxide
Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is available for anxious adults and children for $60 per visit. You stay awake and can usually drive home 15 minutes afterwards. We do not offer IV sedation or general anaesthesia.

## Wheelchair access
The practice is on the ground floor with step-free access, an accessible restroom, and one treatment room large enough for patients to stay in their wheelchair for check-ups.

## Sensory needs
We can book quiet first or last appointments with dimmed lights and minimal waiting-room time for patients with sensory sensitivities. Let us know when booking.
