# Children's dentistry

## First visit
We recommend a child's first dental visit by their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable.

## What to expect
Dr. Natarajan leads our children's care. Visits include a gentle exam, a cleaning, fluoride varnish, and advice for parents on brushing and diet. Parents are welcome to stay in the room.

## Sealants
Sealants are thin protective coatings painted onto the back teeth to prevent decay. We usually recommend them when a child's adult molars come through, around ages 6 and 12. They cost $45 per tooth and many insurance plans cover them for children.

## Nervous children
Tell us when you book if your child is anxious. We schedule a longer, unhurried visit, explain each step with child-friendly words, and never force treatment on a distressed child.
