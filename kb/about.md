# About Juniper Lane Dental

## Who we are
Juniper Lane Dental is a family dental practice at 418 Juniper Lane, Riverton. We have treated patients of all ages since 2011. Our team is three dentists, four hygienists and a front-desk team of five. Juniper Lane Dental is a fictional business created for this demo.

## Our dentists
Dr. Maya Okafor (general and cosmetic dentistry, practice owner), Dr. Daniel Reyes (implants and oral surgery) and Dr. Priya Natarajan (children's dentistry and orthodontics). Every new patient sees one of our dentists at their first visit, not only a hygienist.

## Location and parking
We are on the ground floor of the Juniper Lane Medical Building, next to Riverton Public Library. Free parking for patients is in the building's rear lot; take a ticket and we validate it at the front desk. Bus routes 12 and 40 stop directly outside.

## Languages
Our team speaks English, Spanish, Hindi and Tamil. Tell us your preferred language when you book and we will schedule you with a team member who speaks it where possible.
