# Appointments

## How to book
Book online through the "Book a visit" button on our website, call (555) 014-2290 during opening hours, or ask at the front desk. Online booking shows real-time availability for check-ups, cleanings and new-patient exams. Treatments such as crowns or implants are booked after an exam.

## New patients
Your first visit takes about 60 minutes and includes a full exam, X-rays if needed, a cleaning when time allows, and a written treatment plan with costs. Please arrive 10 minutes early, or complete the new-patient form online beforehand.

## What to bring
Photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of medicines you take, and any recent X-rays from another dentist. Children under 18 must come with a parent or legal guardian at their first visit.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments are charged $50, which is waived the first time and in genuine emergencies.

## Running late
If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten your appointment or rebook it, so that we stay on time for the next patient. Call us if you are running late and we will do our best.

## Reminders
We send a text reminder 48 hours before your appointment and an email the day before. Reply C to the text to confirm. You can switch reminders off by telling the front desk.
