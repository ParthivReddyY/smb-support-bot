# Opening hours

## Regular hours
Monday to Thursday 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Friday 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, every second and fourth Saturday of the month only. Closed on Sundays.

## Early and late appointments
We keep our 8:00 am and 5:00 pm weekday slots for patients who work or study. These book up about two weeks ahead, so reserve them early.

## Public holidays
We are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. On other public holidays we keep normal hours.

## Lunch break
The front desk is staffed through lunch, but no treatments are scheduled between 12:30 pm and 1:15 pm on weekdays.
