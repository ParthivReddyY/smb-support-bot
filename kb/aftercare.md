# Aftercare

## After a filling
Avoid hot drinks and chewing until the numbness wears off, usually 2 to 3 hours, so you do not bite your cheek. Mild sensitivity to cold for a few days is normal. Call us if your bite feels high or pain lasts more than a week.

## After an extraction
Bite on the gauze for 30 minutes. For 24 hours, do not rinse, spit forcefully, smoke or drink through a straw, so the blood clot can form. Eat soft foods and take pain relief as advised by your dentist. Call us if bleeding is heavy after 4 hours or pain gets worse after day three.

## After whitening
Avoid coffee, tea, red wine, curry and smoking for 48 hours. Temporary sensitivity is common and usually settles within two days; a sensitivity toothpaste helps.

## After a crown
Your new crown is fully set when you leave. Floss as normal. If the crown feels loose or your bite feels uneven after a few days, call us for a quick adjustment, which is free of charge.
